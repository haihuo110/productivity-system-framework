# Dachen intelligent manufacturing standards reference

Canonical knowledge base: `haihuo110/dachen-knowledge-base/standards/intelligent-manufacturing-standards.md`

Index: `haihuo110/dachen-knowledge-base/standards/standards-index.json`

Use this reference when orchestrating Dachen Education work involving robot cells, SMT/Industry 4.0 systems, digital twins, virtual simulation training, vocational education or workstation safety. The canonical matrix distinguishes mandatory GB requirements from GB/T, industry, MOE/MOHRSS and contractual references. Before generating a tender, design release, acceptance plan or training claim, require verification of the authoritative full text, current edition, scope and project applicability. In particular, route GB/T 20867, GB/T 37414, SJ/T 10670, GB/T 40647, GB 24541 and the MOE virtual-simulation guideline through compliance review rather than treating the index entry as a legal conclusion.

Operational expectation: attach risk assessment, safety-function/PL evidence, electrical test records, FAT/SAT, competency and internship controls, PPE matrix and LOTO evidence to relevant jobs.
