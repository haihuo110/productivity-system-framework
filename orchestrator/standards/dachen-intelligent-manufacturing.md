# Dachen intelligent manufacturing standards reference

Canonical knowledge base: `haihuo110/dachen-knowledge-base/standards/intelligent-manufacturing-standards.md`

Index: `haihuo110/dachen-knowledge-base/standards/standards-index.json`

Use this reference when orchestrating Dachen Education work involving robot cells, SMT/Industry 4.0 systems, digital twins, virtual simulation, vocational education, lab tenders, field-engineer programs, OT/IT integration, PLC/fieldbus networking, industrial cybersecurity, machine vision or AI manufacturing.

## Direct generation and audit rules

1. Lab and tender generation must check both vocational training-condition standards: industrial robot technology and intelligent manufacturing equipment technology. Include workstation counts, equipment set ratios, electrical load and redundancy, ESD, environmental controls, safety, acceptance and maintenance evidence. Use JY/T teaching-instrument requirements where applicable.
2. Field-engineer program generation must include the five-department program’s core monitoring evidence: work-study alternation ratio, dual-mentor coverage and authentic project practice. For national industry-education communities and municipal industry-education consortia, check governance, enterprise participation, shared bases/resources, curriculum, employment and evaluation records against the applicable guide.
3. OT/IT and digital-twin designs must identify applicable GB/T 33863 (IEC 62541) OPC UA architecture, GB/T 19582 Modbus networking and GB/T 25105 / IEC 61158 fieldbus profiles, including PROFINET/EtherCAT applicability. Require asset/data-flow inventory, information model, topology, PLC/register configuration, diagnostics, timing and virtual-to-physical validation.
4. Industrial-control generation and audit must check GB/T 30976.1/.2 and applicable GB/T 22239 等保2.0 industrial-control extension requirements. Require classification/applicability decision, zones/conduits, identity/access, patch and backup, logging, incident response, vulnerability and assessment evidence.
5. Machine-vision and AI manufacturing work must check GB/T 38892 and GB/T 40685. Require representative labeled data, defect taxonomy, camera/lighting specification, accuracy/repeatability and false-positive/negative acceptance, model/data/version traceability, drift and security controls.
6. Treat the three 1+X documents as “存量参考标准”: 工业机器人应用编程、智能制造系统集成应用、工业机器人操作与运维. Use them for competency/task/assessment mapping only; verify current versions and separately satisfy mandatory or contractually applicable requirements.

## Reference categories

- Training conditions and bids: 《高等职业学校工业机器人技术专业实训教学条件建设标准》; 《高等职业学校智能制造装备技术专业实训教学条件建设标准》; JY/T 教学仪器设备行业标准体系.
- Field engineer and integration: 教育部等五部门《职业教育现场工程师专项培养计划》核心监测指标; 国家级行业产教融合共同体 and 市域产教联合体建设指南及评价标准.
- Interoperability: GB/T 33863 (IEC 62541), GB/T 19582, GB/T 25105 / IEC 61158.
- Security and compliance: GB/T 30976.1, GB/T 30976.2, GB/T 22239 industrial-control extensions.
- Vision and AI: GB/T 38892, GB/T 40685.
- Existing baseline: robot safety GB 11291.1/.2, machinery/risk GB/T 15706, functional safety GB/T 16855/ISO 13849, electrical GB 5226.1, maturity GB/T 39116/.17, relevant MOE/MOHRSS vocational and internship provisions, PPE and LOTO controls.

## Mandatory orchestration behavior

Before producing a tender, design release, acceptance plan, curriculum, audit report or training claim, require verification of authoritative full text, current edition, scope and project applicability. Never turn a GB/T, JY/T, ministry, industry or 1+X reference into an unconditional legal claim. Attach a standards register, applicability decision, evidence map and unresolved verification items to the generated artifact. Safety, cybersecurity and physical electrical controls remain required even where a virtual model or simulation is used.

Operational evidence expected where relevant: URS and tender matrix; lab layout/capacity and ratio calculations; equipment and load schedules; ESD/environment records; risk assessment and safety functions; FAT/SAT; network topology and protocol configuration; OT/IT data-flow and OPC UA model; cybersecurity asset/zone/access/backup/log evidence; vision dataset and performance report; AI model/data version registry; dual-mentor/work-study/authentic-project records; competency mapping; internship, PPE and LOTO controls.
